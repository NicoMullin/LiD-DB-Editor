# Tengoku Enjoyer

**By ZeroUnderOne** — included with the manager with their permission.

Tengoku no longer ends at 450F: it goes on to **1000F**. That is 550 more
floors, each with its escalators, drops, mushrooms, beasts and items, plus the
bosses and Haters to fill them, and the four routes go on from 140F to 250F.
ZeroUnderOne also retuned the floors below, and put back some gear values from
before the offline update.

## Version

Pick one under **Version** in Configuration:

| Version             | What you get                                                             |
|---------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Original**        | the whole mod, with the first release's Haters                           |
| **Route Pure**      | the whole mod, where every Hater carries a weapon from its tower's route |
| **Pick and choose** | only the parts you tick, below                                           |

## Pick and choose

With **Pick and choose**, each part is its own switch. All are on to begin
with:

| Part                                     | What it does                                                                                       |
|------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Haters**                               | which version's Haters (Original or Route Pure) the parts below bring                              |
| **Tengoku to 1000F**                     | the 550 new floors and the routes to 250F, with their escalators, drops, bosses and Haters         |
| **New Haters on floors 51 to 451**       | ZeroUnderOne's Haters on the floors the game already has, and what they drop on the routes         |
| **Tougher enemies on floors 145 to 450** | enemies hit harder and take more on 62 of those floors                                             |
| **Retuned bosses**                       | the bosses' higher levels and the Four Forcemen, retuned to lead into the new floors' bosses       |
| **Gear from before the offline update**  | Hell Ride, Strike Bomb, the M2G-87, the Red Napalm knife and the Armor Breaking Flail S, as before |

## Pre-Nerf Gear

The gear values are also a mod of their own, **Pre-Nerf Gear**, for players
who want only those. Tengoku Enjoyer already has them, so there is no need to
tick both; the manager says so if you do.

## Before you switch it off

Do not switch off Tengoku Enjoyer, or its **Tengoku to 1000F** part, while a
fighter is on a floor past 451F (or past 140F on a route): those floors stop
existing, and the game cannot put the fighter back on one that is not there.
Bring the fighter home first.

Tick it and click **Save Mod List**. Unticking it puts Tengoku back as it was.

Made for game version 1.92.
