# Online Research Costs

**Data by S3er0i9ng** — included with the manager with their permission.

When LET IT DIE went offline, equipment research got much cheaper. This mod
puts 1,017 research recipes back to what they cost online, read by S3er0i9ng
from the PS4 online game's 4.18 parameters.

- **Online material counts**: how many of each material crafting a part and
  each upgrade take. For most recipes the online amount is two to four times the
  offline one.
- **Online SPLithium costs**: what crafting and upgrades cost in SPLithium. Most
  are double; a few are half.

Each is its own switch in Configuration, and both are on to begin with.
Recipes that cost the same online, and the 61 the offline game added, are left
as they are. Nothing else about research changes: what can be researched, when it
opens, and how long it takes stay the same.

For the gear values from before the offline update too, tick **Pre-Nerf Gear**.

Every offline value was checked against the stock database of game 1.92 when
the mod was built.

Tick it and click **Save Mod List**. Unticking it puts the offline costs back.
