# Nitro Boost EXP

Nitro Boost and Turbo-charged Engine give *your chosen*% EXP instead of 40% / 10%, and their in-game descriptions are rewritten to say so.

## Your value

**EXP bonus** - default **100,000%**, anything from 1% to 1,000,000%.

Select this mod and open the **Configuration** tab on the right to change it, or right-click it and pick **Change values...**. Press Enter or click away, then **Save Mod List**.

Stock is 40% for Nitro Boost and 10% for Turbo-charged Engine. The descriptions are rewritten to match, in six languages.

## What it changes

`master_skill.val0` on `SKL_EXPUP_02` (Nitro Boost) and `SKL_EXPUP_01` (Turbo-charged Engine), plus their descriptions.

The descriptions are rewritten with your number in English, German, Spanish, French, Italian and Portuguese, each written the way that language writes numbers. Japanese, Chinese and Korean keep the stock wording.

The descriptions are a separate part, so you can switch them off and keep the number.

Replaces `nitro-boost-100000pct`.

## Reverting

Automatic. The manager keeps the rows it is about to change before it changes them, so unticking the mod and saving puts the stock values back - whatever you had it set to.
