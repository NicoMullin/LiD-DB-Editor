# Bag Size

Every fighter's bag holds more, by as much as you choose.

At stock a bag holds 18 to 54 slots. The fighter's class, grade and limit break
decide it, and Collectors get the most. Pick how the mod changes that in
Configuration:

| How                                             | What it does                                                             | Default  |
|-------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------|----------|
| **Every fighter gets at least this many**       | Every bag holds at least that many (20 to 60); a bigger stock bag stays  | 60 slots |
| **Each fighter gets this many more than stock** | Every bag gets that many more (+1 to +42), so bags still grow with grade | +20      |

Either way, no fighter ends up with fewer slots than stock, and no bag goes
over 60. The Express Pass adds its 10 slots on top, so a bag holds up to 70
with it. 60 is what the well-known 60-slot bag mod gives every fighter, tested
in game; bigger bags go wrong, so 60 is also the most. This is the same idea,
with the number up to you.

Tick it and click **Save Mod List**. Unticking it puts every bag back to its
stock size. Take anything extra out of a fighter's bag first, so nothing is
over the stock size when you switch it off.
